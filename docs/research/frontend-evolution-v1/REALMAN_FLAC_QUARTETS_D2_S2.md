After actual archive-byte SHA256 verification, use only the SHA-bound local system `7z 23.01` tool in structured `l -slt` mode to inventory safe RAR member paths and declared per-member compressed/uncompressed sizes; reject unsafe, duplicated, encrypted, non-regular, oversized or ambiguous records. Choose **exactly one lexically first complete static quartet** and **one lexically first complete moving quartet** by original safe member name/stem **before reading individual FLAC bytes, sample counts, lengths, speech scores or angles**. If the earliest complete quartet is too large, invalid or inconsistent, **reject it rather than skipping to a later one**. Gym was chosen previously only as smallest *remotely declared* archive size, not for high BF/DOA quality and not as D0's official test-scene selection.

- **Exactly 8 files maximum**, original compressed FLAC *file bytes* not decoded PCM: **32 MiB max each, 128 MiB max total**; bounded 75s per single-file read-only 7z streaming extraction and 180s overall extraction budget. A known preinstalled, hash-checked `7z` using exact member names, no wildcard support (`-spd`), and binary stdout (`-so`) feeds first-party Python into flat temporary filenames; neither original archive paths nor links can determine extracted output paths. No other archive or external tool is fetched.
- Original FLAC file SHA256, on-disk byte count, filename/stem SHA and FLAC magic / first **STREAMINFO 34 bytes** are checked, then separately rehashed/read back. Capture **actual native sample rate, encoded channels, bits/sample, total sample count** and PCM-MD5 declaration from STREAMINFO only. This does **NOT decode FLAC frames, authenticate decoded PCM or establish channel audio-clock synchronization**.
- Within each role, reject four channels if native rate, bits or declared total sample count do not match. Mark `native_48khz_all=false` when any format is different; do not automatically resample or label it a 48-kHz capture. If the selected member is absent, encrypted, malformed, too large, or any lister/stream/readback is unsupported, fail closed and retain exact source-only metadata reason. No content-based substitute.
- Log only experiment role, channel, hashed member names/stems and FLAC-header/length/hash aggregates; raw filenames may remain internal to the runner but are **never uploaded**. The public CI artifact has no `*.rar`, `*.flac`, WAV/PCM, audio recordings, individual signal frames or original CSVs.
